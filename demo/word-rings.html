// Gameplay

How to Play

01
See the Rings
Each puzzle shows concentric letter rings. Your job is to align them.
02
Rotate to Align
Tap or drag each ring. Find the angle where letters form a word reading inward to outward.
03
Find All Words
Some puzzles need multiple word alignments in different directions. Find them all to finish.
04
Beat the Clock
Time yourself and compete on the global leaderboard for fastest solve.
