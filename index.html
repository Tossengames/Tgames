How I Work

From idea to
playable game.

01
Concept & Scope
Understanding what the game is, what makes it fun, and what's realistic to build. Getting the core mechanic right first.
02
Prototype
Building a rough playable version fast. Finding the problems early and figuring out what actually works.
03
Build & Iterate
Developing the full game with regular feedback loops. Systems, UI, content — built and tested in layers.
04
Ship
Polishing and releasing. Making sure the game runs well, feels good, and is ready for real players.
